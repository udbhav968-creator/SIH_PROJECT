the ledger

Each row is one physical defect after deduplication. The confirmation count is how many separate buses reported it.

why the raw sightings are kept

Storing only merged defects loses the evidence that merging happened — and that evidence is exactly what a contractor disputes. Every raw sighting is written with a foreign key to the defect it merged into, so the dedup rate below is a database query, not a counter that can drift from reality.
