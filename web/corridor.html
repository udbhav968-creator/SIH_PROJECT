repair order — Priority Index

PI = w1·(100 − PCI) + w2·Vol + w3·Traffic, each term on 0–100. No ward, road name or "VIP" input: the order follows measured damage and use. Click a row to go to it on the map.

live feedconnecting…

Bus positions, defects, IMU-only shocks and work orders as they arrive. Buses send sealed (AES-256-GCM) packets; a bus that was out of coverage catches up when it reconnects. A pothole found on the Inspection page can be added here too.

    why the raw sightings are kept

    Storing only merged defects loses the evidence that merging happened — and that evidence is exactly what a contractor disputes. Every raw sighting is written with a foreign key to the defect it merged into, so the dedup rate is a database query, not a counter that can drift from reality.
