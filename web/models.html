What these numbers do not say

  • Accuracy is dominated by three classes that hold almost every test image. macro-F1 is the number that reflects the rare ones, and it is much lower. Both are shown.
  • Segmentation IoU in the 0.15–0.25 band is a working model, not a solved problem. It replaces a bounding box that had no measured accuracy at all, and a box around a diagonal crack overstates its area by roughly 13×.
  • The IMU classifier's 100% is on simulated accelerometer windows. It means the code works; it says nothing about a real vehicle.
  • Photographs are Brazilian and Kaggle sourced. No Indian road data yet.
