What these numbers do not say

  • Accuracy is dominated by three classes that hold almost every test image. macro-F1 is the number that reflects the rare ones, and it is much lower. Both are shown.
  • Segmentation IoU (above) is a working model, not a solved problem, and it is measured on DNIT (Brazilian) photographs. On clean roads the segmenter still draws false blobs; the classifier in front of it limits the damage. It replaces a bounding box that had no measured accuracy at all, and a box around a diagonal crack overstates its area by roughly 13×.
  • The IMU classifier is trained on real drive logs, but only ten of them, from one vehicle on Indian roads. It is not yet validated on a bus fleet.
  • Indian road photographs come from RDD2022 (smartphone images). The Indian-roads number is measured on crops from held-out RDD2022 India photographs (split by photograph — the official test split has no public labels), which never enter training, and it covers only three classes (normal, crack, pothole). None of the photographs were taken from a bus.
  • The test set is held out by source photograph, but the benchmark's pipeline photographs on the home page are drawn from the same corpus. They demonstrate the pipeline; they are not a second accuracy figure.
