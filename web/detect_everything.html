honest by design

People, vehicles and crossings come from pretrained detectors used as published. The road-damage boxes are an unverified candidate — the detector has not passed this repository's verification gate, so every pothole/crack box is drawn dashed and tagged candidate. Counts are exact counts of the boxes drawn, not a validated accuracy. Nothing here is fabricated. See docs/DETECTION_PIPELINE.md.
