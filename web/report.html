1 · photograph

Stand back so the whole defect is in the frame. The photograph is analysed and then discarded: only the defect, its size and the location are kept.

2 · where is it?

No location yet.

Photographs taken with location turned on carry their own position, which is used first. Otherwise, take the picture where the defect is and use your location.

3 · send
what happens next

The engine looks for a pothole or crack in your photograph. If it finds one, the defect is pinned on the road map. It joins the city's repair list as soon as a bus on its route sees the same defect, or an operator reviews it. Reports that only one person ever sees cannot fill the repair list on their own, which keeps it free of spam.
