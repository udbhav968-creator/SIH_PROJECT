how it works

Every two seconds the phone sends one 640-pixel frame, its GPS fix and the accelerometer readings since the last frame. The engine runs the same logic as the bus unit (edge/bus_agent.py): a defect the camera sees ahead is checked against the second in which the wheels reach it. Felt and seen, it joins the repair ledger. Seen but not felt, it waits until a second vehicle sees the same thing. A frame the input check calls unusable or not a road is never reported.

The frame is analysed and dropped; it is not stored. Fixes worse than ±30 m are not used for defects. Frames are not queued while there is no signal: they are skipped. Not yet tested on a real drive. A phone in a holder shakes differently from a sensor bolted to a bus, so treat its shock readings as indicative.
