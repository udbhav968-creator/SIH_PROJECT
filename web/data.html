Integrity controls

what stops a number being flattering rather than true
near-duplicate rejection

Every incoming image is perceptually hashed (64-bit DCT pHash) and dropped if it is within 8 bits of anything already held. Several Kaggle pothole sets are re-uploads of each other; without this the same photograph lands in both training and test.

re-encode at q80≤ 2 bits
half-size re-upload≤ 8 bits
genuinely different photos≥ 18 bits (5th pct)

Threshold set to 8 — measured over 60 photographs, not guessed.

label-conflict audit

Finds photographs filed under more than one class. This was the bug that held accuracy at 36.6%: 20 photographs, 2,006 files, sitting under three contradictory labels at once.

conflicting photographs found20
files quarantined2,006
accuracy after the fix36.6% → 79.4%

Run by scripts/fix_label_conflicts.py, pinned by a test.

grouped splitting

Train/test is split by source photograph, not by file. An augmented copy can never land on the other side of the split from its original — which would inflate accuracy by tens of points and is the single most common way a road-defect model reports a number it has not earned.

what the data does not cover
  • Indian road photographs come from RDD2022 smartphone images — none were taken from a bus.
  • No night, heavy rain or glare conditions in any quantity.
  • The four rare classes have roughly 50 photographs each — no architecture fixes that.
  • IMU data are real drive logs from a car on Indian roads, not from a bus-mounted unit.
  • No pothole depth ground truth: depth comes from a model, not a measurement.
